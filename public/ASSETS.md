# DALUYAN illustration attribution

landscape-1.svg through landscape-6.svg are original vector landscape illustrations created for this project. They are illustrative, not photographs or evidence of conditions at any particular destination. No external stock photography, sacred media, or third-party logos are included. OpenStreetMap attribution is rendered on the map; map tile service terms still apply.

The geometric Moriones mask, butterfly, wave divider, and Marinduque mosaic in `src/components/CulturalArt.tsx` are original decorative UI illustrations. They are not official festival insignia or destination photographs. Campaign colors were visually matched to the user-provided reference.

Self-hosted Fredoka (600) and DM Sans (400, 600) Latin WOFF2 files were obtained from the corresponding Fontsource 5.3.0 packages through the npm registry. Their SIL Open Font License texts are retained alongside the fonts in `public/fonts`.

`textile-1.svg` through `textile-3.svg` are original flat geometric diamond/weave patterns inspired broadly by Philippine textiles. They do not reproduce or claim to represent any specific Indigenous community's textile. Demo feed media uses these decorative placeholders; real uploaded media remains unchanged.
