## Explicit simulations and fixture content

- Three congestion presets and user-adjusted capacity/demand are **DEMO / SIMULATED**, not live visitor counts or official transport schedules.
- Eight businesses and twelve community posts are fictional testing fixtures. They are visibly labeled and are not actual endorsements, customer reviews, business contacts or authenticated production activity.
- Six original landscape illustrations stand in for media; they are **not photographs** of the named destinations.
- The six destination references cover all municipalities but remain **UNVERIFIED**. Fees are null and source dates unchecked. Automatic recommendations intentionally return no destinations from these unreviewed records. The algorithm's multi-stop output is verified using clearly defined reviewed test fixtures.
- RINI is a local curated lookup system, not an external LLM or an official authority.

## Known implementation limits / further engineering

- Data loads are capped at 500 accessible posts/events; incremental feed display works within this window. Server cursor pagination/aggregate counters and Realtime reconnection monitoring are needed for larger deployments.
- The source registry supports add/approve/withdraw. Human review still decides whether references are authoritative; evidence URLs cannot establish authenticity by themselves.
- No optional review-helpfulness voting, PWA/offline mode, AR demonstration, payments/micro-tipping or audit hash-chain is included. Core audit events are centralized database records, never a blockchain claim.
- No exact travel ETA, road routing, live ferry departures, booking or checkout is supplied.
- Business image galleries, self-service listing photo uploads, rich cultural-media approval editing, automated account erasure, upload malware scanning and orphan-storage cleanup are not implemented. Existing listing images can be replaced through trusted reviewed data; social/profile/feedback image upload flows are implemented. Use approved media and document storage-retention procedures before production.
- Automated tests do not substitute for manual screen-reader, real-device, hosted-provider or deployed-browser testing. The full 16-step acceptance scenario remains **unrun against a live backend**.
